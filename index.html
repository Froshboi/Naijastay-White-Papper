01 — The Problem

Three Broken Markets.
One Common Pain.

Most Nigerians search for houses and hotels on their phones. Yet there is no platform that solves all three problems with real trust.

🏠 Apartments & Rentals

Finding a rental apartment in Nigeria is expensive and full of risk. Agents charge high inspection fees just to view a property, and often demand agency and legal fees that can equal months of rent. Many add hidden fees after you commit. Fake listings are common. You pay for a house you never see. Landlords deal with unserious tenants who waste time and disappear. There is no protection when deals go wrong and no accountability for bad actors.

Click to expand ↓

🏨 Hotels

Booking hotels comes with its own stress for both strangers and locals. Travelers from other cities often arrive to find their online booking was fake or the hotel is overbooked with no refund. Locals booking for short stays, events, or emergencies face price hikes on arrival and rooms that do not match the photos. Customer service is unresponsive when issues arise. There is no single trusted place to compare verified hotels and know your money is safe.

Click to expand ↓

🏗️ Buying & Selling Properties

Buying or selling land and houses is slow, risky, and expensive. Fake documents and land disputes are common. Buyers lose money to sellers who do not actually own the property. The process involves too many middlemen who increase costs and delay transactions. Access to verified ownership records is hard. Getting home loans takes months of paperwork. After paying, buyers often face court cases to claim what they bought.
